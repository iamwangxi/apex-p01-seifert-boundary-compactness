## Richards: standard surface classification

**Ian Richards.** *On the classification of noncompact surfaces*. *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* **106** (1963), 259–269.

**Original article not checked; no pinpoint claim.** Use: the standard fact that a connected simply connected noncompact surface without boundary is a topological plane.
